01 — About

A bit about me

My interest in low-level systems started with microcontrollers and grew into a passion for understanding how software interacts with hardware at the deepest level. On the data side, I'm driven by the challenge of building pipelines that turn output into meaningful, queryable insight. As a Front-End Developer Intern with the Air Force Sustainment Center (402nd SWEG), I focused on building responsive, secure internal web tools to streamline onboarding and onboarding module analytics.

I specialize in connecting the physical world to high-level data solutions at the intersection of embedded systems, data engineering, and operational policy. From developing low-level microcontroller firmware to architecting backend pipelines that analyze sensor streams, I design resilient, end-to-end IoT systems. My background in Political Science (Pre-Law) gives me a distinct analytical advantage, which allows me to translate complex regulatory, security, and operational requirements into clean software.
